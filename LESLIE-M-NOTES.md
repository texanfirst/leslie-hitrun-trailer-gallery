1. **Millis Transfer** — Best combined fit: the official site describes trademark **maroon trucks and crisp white trailers**, and lists an Alvarado, Texas facility; the M-name and red/maroon-on-white clue fit well. Rear-door lettering still needs direct confirmation.
2. **Mesilla Valley Transportation (MVT)** — Official brand guidance documents the MVT initials/globe mark and a red primary logo color; El Paso headquarters and a Laredo terminal make Southwest/Texas travel plausible. The existing gallery photo reads blue, so color/angle remains a caveat.
3. **May Trucking Company** — Existing lineup evidence is red-on-white, and the official site lists a Dallas terminal plus nationwide refrigerated operations. The exact rear-door treatment is not confirmed in the public material reviewed.
4. **P.A.M. Transport** — Red PAM wordmark on white equipment is a reasonable M-shaped-letter possibility and a national fleet is plausible on I-35. The gallery's available image is not a useful modern rear-door example, so this stays a lineup candidate rather than evidence.
5. **Mercer Transportation** — M-name national carrier with varied owner-operator equipment, so a white trailer is possible; no strong public red/burgundy rear-door evidence was found.
6. **Maverick Transportation** — Red branding and an M-name are superficially compatible, but Maverick's current operation is chiefly flatbed, glass, marine, and specialized equipment; a normal white box trailer with rear doors is a weak fit.
7. **Marten Transport** — M-name and white refrigerated trailers are plausible, but the official logo guide specifies blue (with orange accents), and the existing gallery photo is blue rather than burgundy/red.
8. **Melton Truck Lines** — M-name and Texas/Mexico activity keep it in the broad review set, but the current brand is signature Melton blue and the fleet is predominantly flatbed/step-deck, not a white dry van with rear writing.

## Strong elimination hints

- **Marten:** official asset guide limits the logo to Marten blue/white/black usage (with orange palette); a clearly burgundy rear wordmark would be a strong mismatch.
- **Melton:** official fleet description emphasizes blue trucks and open-deck flatbeds/step decks; a conventional enclosed trailer with rear-door writing would be a strong mismatch.
- **Maverick:** official description emphasizes flatbed, glass, marine, and specialized markets; a standard dry-van rear door is unlikely.
- **Prime Inc:** existing gallery photos show white reefers with blue/black Prime lettering, not red/burgundy; a clear red M mark would be a mismatch.
- **Miller Transporters:** existing evidence is blue and tank-oriented; a white dry-van rear door with burgundy M lettering is a poor fit.
- **P.A.M. caveat:** do not treat the available old/public logo or non-matching gallery image as proof of a particular trailer.
